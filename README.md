# Hero Craze — Support

Hero Craze is a match-3 puzzle adventure for iPhone and iPad. Match gems to power a party of five heroes, chain cascades, and fight the bosses standing between you and the castle.

## Contact

Please include your device model and iOS version, and a screenshot if something looks wrong. I'm a solo developer and read every message.

## Common questions

**I reinstalled the game or got a new device. How do I get my purchase back?**
Open the game, tap **OPTIONS** on the main menu, then **STORE → RESTORE**. Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. If Worlds 3+ are still locked after that, email me with the Apple ID's email (not the password) and I'll sort it out.

**Does my progress sync between my iPhone and iPad?**
Yes, through iCloud. Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive turned on. Sync happens when the game opens and when it returns from the background; give it a few seconds on a fresh install.

**I'm out of lives.**
Lives refill on their own, one every 15 minutes, up to five. Boss Rush and Last Stand never cost lives.

**What's included in the free version?**
Worlds 1 and 2 (levels 1–10), plus Boss Rush and Last Stand once you've reached them. The one-time **Full Game Unlock** opens Worlds 3 and up, with more worlds added in free updates. There are no subscriptions, no ads, and no consumables to buy.

**The game asks me to buy something I already own.**
Use **OPTIONS → STORE → RESTORE** as above. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not the device.

## Privacy

Hero Craze does not require an account. The only data that leaves your device is what's needed to process and restore the Full Game Unlock (an anonymous app user ID and your purchase record, handled through Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat) and your game progress, which is stored in your own iCloud account. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking. Full details are on the App Store listing under App Privacy.
